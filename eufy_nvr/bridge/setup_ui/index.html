Eufy NVR Local · setup

Connect your camera recorder

Your NVR is the box your cameras plug into. This setup connects that box to Home Assistant.

Development build — this setup screen is not yet enabled in the installed app. In the guided version, saving a recorder starts its camera service. It does not change recordings.

Before you start

Use the Eufy account that owns the recorder, not a shared guest account. The owner has permission to open its camera feeds.

This is not a fully offline connection. Signing in and arranging the connection still need Eufy's internet service. The app is designed to receive the video over your home network.

Your Eufy password is different from the app's local video password. This screen does not change either password.

For a new guided app, the Home Assistant integration can create the local video password for you after you confirm. You do not need to copy or remember it. That password protects camera video inside your home; it is not sent to Eufy and does not change your Eufy account password.

Opening a protected setup session…

Camera service

A running service means the video software has started. Open a camera tile to check the actual picture. This screen does not change recording settings or restart Home Assistant.

1. Sign in to Eufy

Use the same email you use in the Eufy phone app. This tells Eufy which account owns your recorder.

This proves the account is yours. Normally the setup clears your password after signing in and saves a temporary access token instead.

Optional and off by default. When you tick this and save your recorder, the app keeps your email and password in its private data so it can sign in again if Eufy rejects the saved access token. The app does not encrypt this saved password; someone who can read the app's private files or backups could read it. Protect your backups. A picture check, email code or failed renewal still needs your help. Forget saved sign-in removes it from the current app data, not from older backups, and does not change your Eufy password.

Choose where your Eufy account is registered, not where you are travelling today. For an Australian account, choose Australia. If your country is missing, stop and ask for support; do not choose a different country.

Server group — an advanced setting

This selects the Eufy service we ask to sign you in. It does not change your account country. The Australian installation tested for this project uses US. Keep your known working setting. If you do not know it and sign-in fails, ask for help instead of trying every group repeatedly.

2. Check your email

Eufy needs another check before allowing this sign-in. Look for its six-digit email code, including in your junk folder. Enter it here to prove you can access the account's email.

Do not post the code in screenshots or messages. If it never arrives, cancel and ask for help. Repeated sign-in attempts can temporarily lock the account.

3. Choose your recorder

This chooses which recorder Home Assistant will connect to. It does not erase recordings, move cameras or reset the recorder. Saving replaces this app's saved account connection.

Only supported T8N00 recorders appear. If two have the same name, give them different names in the Eufy app first, then cancel and start again. Do not guess.

Account connection saved

The app now has permission details for your chosen recorder. That is not yet proof that video works.

If a camera-service status appears above, follow that message, then open a camera tile to check the picture. No recording settings were changed.

Setup closes after 10 minutes to avoid leaving account details waiting in memory. Cancel clears the unfinished setup; it does not delete a previously saved connection.
